### 1. [V-STORM: A Structure-Aware Grounded Chain of Thought Dataset for Faithful and Fine-Grained Spatio-Temporal Video Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09511)

**2611.09511** | Dian Jiao, Yuqian Yuan, Jiaqi Zhu, Mingjian Gao, Siwen Tan, Yunqi Cao, Jinhui Li, Sijing Li, Zhenxuan Fan, Haoyang Wang, Wenqiao Zhang, Siliang Tang

> While MLLMs can often identify what happens in a video, they still struggle with fine-grained spatio-temporal reasoning, which requires precise grounding when and where the supporting evidence appears and composing such evidence into a coherent reasoning chain. This challenge is exacerbated by the lack of scalable data construction and evaluation pipelines that provide granular, verifiable supervision linking reasoning steps to explicit spatio-temporal evidence.To address this gap, we present V-STORM, a structure-aware framework that transforms videos into Spatio-Temporal Scene Graphs (STSGs) and derives Grounded Chain of Thought (GCoT) from validated evidence paths, making each reasoning step traceable to structured and verifiable spatio-temporal evidence.Under this framework, we construct a 260K-sample training set and a 3K-sample human-verified benchmark, both featuring GCoT annotations that link reasoning to explicit spatio-temporal evidence.Experiments reveal a clear separation between evidence localization and evidence composition: current MLLMs can often identify relevant spatio-temporal cues, yet still fail to integrate them into correct multi-step reasoning, exposing a bottleneck that answer-only evaluation largely obscures.
